Workspace Notes

✓ cmux · Productivity · Free · syncs with your cmux account
PreviewGet

A Markdown note pinned to each workspace, synced across your machines. Agents in the workspace read it when they start and append what they did, so the next session picks up where the last one stopped.

Why: handoff notes live in scratch files, issues and chat scrollback today; this keeps them next to the work, readable by people and agents alike.
